Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AI Token Tools ("we," "us," or "our"), operated through aitokentools.com, handles information when you use our website and tools. Our fundamental commitment: we do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from your use of our tools.

Information We Do Not Collect

When you use our tools — the Token Counter, API Cost Calculator, Model Comparison, or System Prompt Builder — all computation happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. We do not:

  • Collect, store, or transmit any text you enter into our tools
  • Use cookies for tracking or advertising purposes
  • Create user accounts or store session data
  • Log IP addresses in association with any tool usage
  • Share any data with third parties for advertising purposes

Analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand basic traffic patterns — which pages are visited and from which countries, browser types, and devices. Cloudflare Web Analytics is specifically designed to be privacy-friendly:

  • It does not use cookies of any kind
  • It does not fingerprint individual users
  • It does not collect personally identifiable information
  • It aggregates data at the country level, not by individual IP address
  • Data is processed and retained by Cloudflare in accordance with their privacy policy

You can read Cloudflare's privacy policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Google AdSense

We display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve personalized ads based on your browsing history and interests. Google's advertising practices are governed by their own privacy policies.

  • Google may collect data about your visits to this site and other sites for ad personalization
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at adssettings.google.com
  • You can learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Our site participates in the IAB TCF (Transparency and Consent Framework) for GDPR compliance in the EU

Contact Form

When you use our contact form, submitting the form opens your default email client with a pre-filled message. We do not store or process form submissions on our servers. The contact form uses a mailto: link — your message is sent directly from your email client to our inbox. We retain email correspondence only as long as needed to address your inquiry.

Cookies

AI Token Tools itself does not set any cookies. Cookies may be set by third-party services we use:

  • Google AdSense: Sets cookies for ad serving and frequency capping. These are Google's cookies and are governed by Google's Cookie Policy.
  • Cloudflare: May set a __cf_bm cookie for bot management purposes. This is a security cookie, not a tracking cookie, and expires within 30 minutes of your visit.

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Blocking cookies from Google may affect ad personalization but will not affect the functionality of our tools.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through our contact form, please contact us at the address below and we will promptly delete it.

Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal data through our tools, there is no database of user information to secure. Correspondence sent to us via email is handled with standard email security practices. Our site is served over HTTPS (enforced by Cloudflare) to protect data in transit.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the site after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us via our contact page. We respond to all privacy-related inquiries within 5 business days.
